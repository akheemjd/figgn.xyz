Paint Coverage Guide

  • One gallon covers approximately 350–400 sq ft with one coat.
  • This calculator uses 350 sq ft per gallon for a conservative estimate.
  • Rough or textured surfaces may require more paint.
  • Always buy an extra quart for touch-ups.

FAQs

Should I buy paint by the gallon or 5-gallon bucket?

5-gallon buckets are cheaper per gallon and make sense for large projects (3+ gallons). For smaller rooms, individual gallons give you more flexibility.

Do I need primer?

Primer is recommended for: painting over dark colors, new drywall, stained surfaces, or when switching from oil-based to latex paint. Primer covers ~200-300 sq ft per gallon.
