How to Use This Calculator

  1. Choose mode: Find Pace (distance + time → pace), Find Time (distance + pace → time), or Find Distance (time + pace → distance).
  2. Toggle units: Miles or kilometers.
  3. Enter your values: Complete the visible fields for your selected mode.
  4. Results update instantly — see your pace, speed, and the equivalent per-km pace.

How It's Calculated

Pace = Time ÷ Distance

Example: 25:00 (1,500 sec) ÷ 3.107 mi = 482.8 sec/mi = 8:03/mi

Speed = Distance ÷ Time (in hours)

Example: 3.107 mi ÷ (1,500/3,600) hr = 7.46 mph

When miles is selected, the per-km equivalent is shown for reference. When km is selected, the per-mile equivalent is shown.

FAQ

What's a good running pace?

It depends on your fitness level and distance. A 10:00/mile (6:13/km) is comfortable for many beginners. 8:00/mile (4:58/km) is solid intermediate. Under 6:00/mile (3:44/km) for distance running is competitive. Focus on your own improvement rather than comparing.

How do I use this for race planning?

Use Find Time mode: enter your race distance and your goal pace. The calculator tells you your predicted finish time. Or use Find Pace mode after a training run to find your current pace, then project to longer distances.

Why does my pace differ on a treadmill?

Treadmills are calibrated differently and often read slightly fast or slow. Wind resistance, incline, and belt calibration all affect the actual effort. Use outdoor GPS-tracked runs for the most accurate pace data.
