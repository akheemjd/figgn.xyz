Mulch Coverage Guide

  • 1 cubic yard covers ~108 sq ft at 3 inches deep.
  • 1 bag (2 cu ft) covers ~12 sq ft at 2 inches deep, ~8 sq ft at 3 inches.
  • Bulk mulch is cheaper per cubic yard than bagged — $25–40/cy vs $3–5/bag.
  • Refresh annually: Existing beds typically need 1–2 inches of fresh mulch per year.

FAQs

Bagged vs bulk mulch — which should I buy?

For 5+ cubic yards, bulk delivery is significantly cheaper. For small beds (< 2 cy), bags are more convenient. Bulk mulch costs $25–40 per cubic yard vs $60–100+ for equivalent bagged.

How often should I replace mulch?

Organic mulch breaks down over 1–2 years. Add 1–2 inches annually to maintain depth. Remove old mulch only if it's matted, moldy, or has built up to more than 4 inches.
