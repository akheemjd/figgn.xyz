How is the final exam score calculated?

Formula: (Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight. For example, with 85% current, 90% desired, and 30% final weight: (90 − 85 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = 101.7%.

What if the needed score is over 100%?

It means your desired grade isn't mathematically possible with your current standing and the final's weight. You'd need extra credit, a curve, or a higher weight on the final.

What's the standard grading scale?

The standard US scale: A=90-100, B=80-89, C=70-79, D=60-69, F=below 60. Some schools use plus/minus variants (e.g., A- = 90-93, B+ = 87-89).
