FAQs

What if my school uses a different grading scale?

Different schools use different cutoffs (e.g. 93%+ for an A). This calculator uses the most common standard. Adjust your target based on your school's specific scale.

How is the final exam score calculated?

Formula: (Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight. If the result is over 100%, you may need extra credit to reach your goal.
