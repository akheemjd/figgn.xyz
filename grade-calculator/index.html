FAQ

How is the final exam score calculated?

Formula: Needed Score = (Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight. For example: (85 − 78 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = 101.3% — this means you'd need over 100%, which may not be possible.

What if I need more than 100% on the final?

The calculator shows a warning. This means your current grade is too low relative to your desired grade and the exam weight. Consider adjusting your target or speaking with your instructor about extra credit options.

Is this the same scale used by all schools?

This uses the most common 10-point scale (A=90+, B=80-89, etc.). Some schools use plus/minus grading (A- = 90-93, A = 93-100) or different thresholds. Check your syllabus for the exact scale.
