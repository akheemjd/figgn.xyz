How GPA Is Calculated

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credits. Each letter grade is worth grade points (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0), multiplied by the course credits. Sum all quality points and divide by total credits attempted.

What is a good college GPA?

A 3.0 (B average) is considered good. A 3.5+ is strong for graduate school applications. A 2.0 is typically the minimum to remain in good academic standing.

Weighted vs. unweighted GPA

Unweighted GPA uses a 4.0 scale where A=4.0 regardless of course difficulty. Weighted GPA (often 5.0 scale) gives extra points for honors/AP/IB courses — an A in an AP course might count as 5.0 instead of 4.0.

How is cumulative GPA different?

Semester GPA covers only the current term's courses. Cumulative GPA includes all courses taken across all semesters, weighted by credits.
