FAQ

What's the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?

Unweighted GPA uses a 4.0 scale regardless of course difficulty. Weighted GPA (5.0 scale) gives extra points for honors, AP, or IB courses — an A in an AP class is worth 5.0 instead of 4.0.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credits. Quality points for each course = Grade Points × Credit Hours. Example: A- (3.7) in a 3-credit course = 11.1 quality points.

How do I calculate cumulative GPA?

Cumulative GPA combines all semesters: (Prior GPA × Prior Credits + New Quality Points) ÷ (Prior Credits + New Credits). Enter your prior totals in the cumulative section for automatic calculation.
