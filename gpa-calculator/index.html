Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good GPA?

A 3.0 (B average) is considered good. A 3.5+ (A-/B+ average) is excellent and competitive for most colleges and graduate programs. A 3.7+ puts you in strong contention for honors and competitive admissions.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credit Hours. Each letter grade is worth a number of grade points (A=4.0, B=3.0, etc.), multiplied by the course credit hours. Add all grade points, then divide by total credits.

What's the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?

Unweighted GPA uses a standard 4.0 scale regardless of course difficulty. Weighted GPA gives extra points for honors, AP, or IB courses — typically on a 5.0 scale where an A in an AP class is worth 5.0 instead of 4.0.

Does this calculator work for college GPA?

Yes. Enter your courses, letter grades, and credit hours. The calculator works for any grading system that uses letter grades and credit hours.
