How to Use

  1. Select your room shape — rectangle, L-shaped, or circular.
  2. Enter dimensions in feet. For rectangles: length × width. For L-shapes: overall dimensions and cutout. For circles: diameter.
  3. Adjust waste factor (5-20%). Industry standard is 10% for straight-lay planks, 15% for diagonal/herringbone patterns.
  4. Set box coverage — check your flooring packaging for sq ft per box (commonly 15-30 sq ft).

How It's Calculated

Rectangle: Area = Length × Width

Area = 14 ft × 12 ft = 168 sq ft

L-Shape: Area = (Overall L × Overall W) − (Cutout L × Cutout W)

Area = (20 × 16) − (8 × 6) = 320 − 48 = 272 sq ft

Circle: Area = π × (Diameter ÷ 2)²

Area = π × (15 ÷ 2)² = 176.7 sq ft

Then: Total = Raw Area × (1 + Waste%/100)

Boxes = RoundUp(Total ÷ Box Coverage)

FAQ

How much waste should I add?

Standard recommendation: 10% for straight-lay planks, 15% for diagonal patterns, and 20% for herringbone or rooms with many corners. Always round up to full boxes.

How many sq ft in a box of flooring?

Most laminate and vinyl plank boxes cover 15-30 sq ft. Hardwood typically 20-24 sq ft per box. Check your specific product — it varies by manufacturer.

What if my room isn't a perfect shape?

Break the room into smaller rectangles, calculate each separately, and sum them. For irregular shapes, always add extra waste (15-20%).

Should I measure in inches or feet?

Feet with decimal. For example, 12'6" = 12.5 ft. Divide inches by 12 to convert: 6 inches = 0.5 ft.
