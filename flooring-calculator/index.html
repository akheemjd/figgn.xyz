Why add a waste factor?

Flooring installation requires cuts around corners, doorways, and irregular walls. Even a simple rectangular room wastes 5-10% of material. Complex layouts with angles or patterns may need 15-20% extra.

How many boxes do I need?

Divide the total area (including waste) by the coverage per box, then round up to the nearest whole box. You can't buy a fraction of a box, and running short mid-project means delays and potential dye-lot mismatches.

Rectangle vs. L-Shape vs. Circle

Rectangle: Length × Width. L-Shape: Treat as two rectangles (L1×W1 + L2×W2). Circle: π×(Diameter/2)².
