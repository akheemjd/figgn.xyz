How to Use

  1. Enter the bill amount before tax (or after — your choice).
  2. Select a tip percentage from the presets or enter a custom amount. Standard rates: 15% for average service, 18-20% for good service.
  3. Split the bill by increasing the number of people. Each person's share updates instantly.
  4. Toggle round-up to round the total up to the nearest dollar.

FAQ

Should I tip on pre-tax or post-tax?

Most guidelines recommend tipping on the pre-tax subtotal. However, many people tip on the total including tax — the difference on a typical bill is small. Either way, the calculator works for both.

What's the standard restaurant tip?

In the US and Canada, 15-20% is standard. 15% for adequate service, 18% for good service, 20%+ for excellent service. Some restaurants automatically add gratuity for large parties (usually 6+ people).

Does this work for other services?

Yes. Common tip rates: food delivery 15-20%, bartenders $1-2 per drink or 15-20%, hair stylists 15-20%, taxi/rideshare 15-20%, hotel housekeeping $2-5 per night.
