Standard Tipping Guide

In the United States, standard tipping practices by service:

  • Restaurants (full service): 15–20% of pre-tax bill
  • Delivery: 10–15% or $3–5 minimum
  • Bartenders: $1–2 per drink or 15–20% of tab
  • Rideshare/taxi: 15–20%
  • Hair/beauty services: 15–20%

FAQs

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Standard etiquette is to tip on the pre-tax subtotal. However, the difference is usually small enough that either is acceptable.

How do I split a bill with different amounts per person?

Calculate the total tip, add it to the bill, then divide each person's items separately. This calculator works best when splitting evenly.
