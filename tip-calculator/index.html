Standard restaurant tipping in the US is 15–20% of the pre-tax bill.

Should I tip on pre-tax or post-tax?

Convention is to tip on the pre-tax subtotal. Tipping on tax and service charges inflates the total. Most servers expect 15-20% on the food and drink total before tax.

How do I split a bill with different items?

This calculator splits evenly by head count. For uneven splits (each person pays for their own items), calculate each person's subtotal separately and add their share of the tip and tax.
