Effective date: August 3, 2026

figgn.xyz ("figgn," "we," "us") operates a collection of free online calculators. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

Automatically collected information

When you visit figgn.xyz, our advertising partners and analytics services may automatically receive standard technical information sent by your browser, including your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited, and time spent on each page. We use this information to understand how people find and use the site.

Information you provide

figgn calculators process your input entirely in your browser. The numbers you type into any calculator are never sent to our servers or stored by us. We do not collect, see, or retain the values you calculate.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: We store your light / dark theme preference in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your choice on return visits.
  • Advertising: Third-party advertising partners, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to figgn.xyz and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing activity across the web.

Advertising

figgn.xyz displays advertisements served by Google AdSense and potentially other third-party ad networks. These ad providers may use cookies and web beacons to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services that may be of interest to you.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EU users)

You can also disable cookies in your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of figgn.xyz and other websites.

How we use information

We use automatically collected information to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Understand site traffic and usage patterns
  • Detect and prevent abuse or invalid traffic
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect or store the numbers you calculate.
