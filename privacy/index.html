Privacy Policy

Effective 3 August 2026

figgn.xyz is a set of free calculators. This page explains what happens to information when you use them, in plain terms.

What we do not collect

Everything you type into a calculator stays in your browser. Bill amounts, grades, room measurements and every other input are processed by JavaScript on your own device and are never transmitted to us, stored on a server, or logged. We have no accounts, no sign-up, and no contact forms.

Cookies and local storage

We store one preference locally: your choice of light or dark mode, saved in your browser's localStorage. A dismissed advertisement is remembered for the current session in sessionStorage. Neither is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes both.

Advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits.
  • We do not have access to, and do not control, the cookies used by advertisers.

Opting out

You can opt out of personalised advertising:

  • Google Ads Settings — adssettings.google.com
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US) — optout.aboutads.info
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EU) — youronlinechoices.eu
  • Network Advertising Initiative — optout.networkadvertising.org

Opting out stops personalisation. It does not remove advertising.

Analytics

If analytics are in use on this site, they are configured to record aggregate page views only, with IP addresses anonymised. No calculator inputs are ever included.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete — but if you believe a child has provided information through a third-party advertisement, contact the advertiser or use the opt-out links above.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact
