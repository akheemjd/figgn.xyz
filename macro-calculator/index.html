How It's Calculated

First, BMR is calculated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. That is multiplied by your activity level to get TDEE. Then the calorie target is adjusted by your goal (maintain, lose 500, or gain 500).

BMR = Mifflin-St Jeor (weight, height, age, sex)
TDEE = BMR × activity multiplier
Target = TDEE + goal adjustment (floored at BMR)

Default macro split: 30% protein, 40% carbs, 30% fat. Protein is 4 calories per gram, carbs 4, fat 9. So for a 2,271-calorie target: protein = 2,271 × 0.30 ÷ 4 = 170 g, carbs = 2,271 × 0.40 ÷ 4 = 227 g, fat = 2,271 × 0.30 ÷ 9 = 76 g.

Protein is the priority. Hit that number first, let carbs and fat fill the rest. Within those, the exact ratio matters far less than total calories for weight change.
