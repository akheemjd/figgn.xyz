How is weighted average calculated?

Weighted Average = ∑(Value × Weight) ÷ ∑(Weight). Each value matters in proportion to its weight. A value of 90 with weight 50 contributes 4,500 to the numerator, while 78 with weight 20 contributes 1,560.

Weighted vs. simple average

A simple average treats all values equally. A weighted average gives more importance to values with higher weights. For example, if you score 90 on a test worth 50% of your grade and 80 on one worth 10%, your weighted average is 88.3, but your simple average is 85.
