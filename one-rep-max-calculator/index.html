1RM estimates are approximations. Never attempt a true max without proper warm-up, spotting and technique. These formulas become less accurate above 10 reps.

How It's Calculated

Each formula estimates what you could lift for a single rep from what you lifted for multiple reps:

Epley:      1RM = w × (1 + r/30)
Brzycki:    1RM = w × 36 / (37 − r)
Lombardi:  1RM = w × r0.10
O'Conner: 1RM = w × (1 + r/40)
w = weight lifted, r = reps performed

Worked example (Epley): 225 lb for 5 reps → 225 × (1 + 5/30) = 225 × 1.167 = 263 lb estimated 1RM.
