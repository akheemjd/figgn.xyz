**How it works:**
- Wall measurements: 4 walls, each in feet (pre-fill with 12, 14, 12, 14)
- Ceiling height: default 8ft
- Doors: stepper (each subtracts 20 sq ft), default 1
- Windows: stepper (each subtracts 15 sq ft), default 2
