How do you calculate a percentage?

The basic formula: (Part ÷ Whole) × 100 = Percentage. For example, 30 out of 120 is (30/120)×100 = 25%.

What's percentage increase vs. decrease?

Percentage change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100. A positive result means an increase, negative means a decrease. Going from 50 to 75 is a 50% increase; from 75 to 50 is a 33.3% decrease.
