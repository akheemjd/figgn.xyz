FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. Example: 20% of 150 = 150 × (20 ÷ 100) = 150 × 0.20 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Percentage change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100. If the result is positive, it's an increase. If negative, it's a decrease. Example: from 50 to 75 = (75−50)÷50×100 = 50% increase.

What's the difference between percentage and percentage points?

If a rate goes from 10% to 15%, the increase is 5 percentage points, but the percentage increase is 50% (because 5÷10×100 = 50%). Be careful not to confuse these — they mean very different things.
